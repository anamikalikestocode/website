ai is not going to take your job

In 1930, Keynes - yes the very smart economist - predicted the average person would work 15 hours a week by 2030. He thought technology would make us so rich and satisfied that we'd barely need to work. Today, in 2026, the average American works 41 hours a week. We are no outlier to the people who went through the industrial revolution or the invention of the computer. We will be ok.
